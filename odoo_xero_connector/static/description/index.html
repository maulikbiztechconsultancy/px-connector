    Connecting your Xero account with Odoo!

    Odoo Xero Connector is a flawless solution to perfectly connect the two amazing solutions that are Odoo as well as Xero. The module allows you to import and export all the important data to manage the accounting.

    Import/Export Functions for Sale/Purchase Orders

    Odoo Xero Connector facilitates you to easily import/export the Sale Orders and Purchase Orders.

    The module allows you to import as well as export the partners, products, sale orders from Odoo to Xero and vice versa.

    Import/Export Functions for Accounting Data

    Odoo Xero Connector facilitates you to easily import/export the Accounting Data.

    The module allows you to import the Accounts, Customer Invoices, Vendor Bills, Credit Notes, Vendor Credit Notes and Payments from Xero to Odoo.

    You can export Accounts, Customer Invoices, Vendor Bills, Credit Notes, Vendor Credit Notes and Payments from Odoo to Xero.
